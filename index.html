FAQ

5 answers
Is Gadgetsforall affiliated with AliExpress?

No. Gadgetsforall is an independent project. AliExpress and all other brand names belong to their respective owners.

How do you decide which products are the best?

Each listing gets a score based on price, buyer rating, sales volume, store age and reputation, and how closely the item matches its description. New stores, low sales and poor ratings are penalized.

Do you check shipping costs?

Yes, on the finalists. Shipping varies by country and seller, so we verify it on the top picks before recommending them.
